Why some detector findings remain

Named channel recipes intentionally use cream surfaces and subtle glows. Foundation specimens also contain sequential reference labels, and social display text uses tighter leading than body copy. These need contextual review, not blanket suppression. Remaining quality warnings are not certified as false positives.

See channel profiles for the exact exceptions.

Migration notes

Forms must explicitly pass type="submit". Author pages should request headingAs="h1"; author footers now default to h2. The canonical red alias and blue button fill changed. Consumers relying on full style replacement should review their overrides.

Read the complete consumption and upgrade guide.

Scope left with the brand owner

Illustration approval and provenance cannot be inferred from local files. The asset catalog marks these unknowns explicitly. No assets were deleted and no approval was invented. Existing presentation and guidance edits were retained.

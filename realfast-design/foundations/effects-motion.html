UsageRestrained motion. State changes use standard (200ms); entrances use out (360ms); larger moves use in-out. Data reveals fire once when a chart scrolls into view (IntersectionObserver, ~35% visible), never on a loop — the looping here is demo-only. Motion should be felt, not seen: the whole build finishes inside ~1.5s, then the chart holds its final state. Bars and fills grow from the baseline via transform:scaleY or animated width with --ease-out over 650–900ms, siblings staggered 90–130ms so the eye tracks the build; lines draw on via stroke-dashoffset with --ease-in-out (~1.4s) and the end label lands last; donut slices sweep clockwise largest-first; headline numbers count up over ~1.6s with a long settle. Sequence by meaning — context first, the point last — and keep the red accent the final thing to resolve. One animated chart per viewport; neighbours reveal as they arrive, never all at once. Always respect prefers-reduced-motion — everything collapses to its final state and the static chart stays the source of truth.

How to use this template

Bring the author’s raw material in any format - call notes, a transcript, a metrics sheet, a rough paragraph - and map each piece onto the fixed spine below. You don’t have to write in order; fill sections as the content fits, then delete this panel.

Stay original to the source content. Use the author’s words as close to verbatim as possible - don’t paraphrase, condense away details, or add narrative of your own. The only edits allowed are the house rules (hyphens for dashes, realfast lowercase italic, sentence case). If the source has section detail (setup, tooling, lists, learnings), carry it over in full rather than summarising.

The spine: Hero › The problem › Why it mattered › What we did › The build › The metric moved › In their words › What’s next.

Lead with the outcome. Never open on the process. The single biggest metric goes in the hero headline and again, larger, in The metric moved. Every metric is shown as a large number, never buried in prose.

The metric moved is the signature moment: use the before / after comparison bars plus the “how it moved” steps already built into that section. Swap in the real numbers.

Imagery. One hero visual, 16:9. Generate it from the MidJourney prompt card in the hero (Copy button), then replace the card with the image. Do the same for any supporting visual. All art follows the muted illustration style (see Brand › Illustration).

Charts & data. If the source has a chart, recreate it exactly - same title, subtitle, series, totals and per-week values - never approximate or invent data points. Restyle it to the system: a light ground (never a black band), the pale-blue › dusty-blue › slate-navy value ramp with --success for the completed series (see Charts › Stacked bar chart), totals set above each bar, legend below. Never invent chart colours, and never use black or signal red as a data series.

In their words is optional. Include the quote block only if the client actually gave a quote - never fabricate or paraphrase one. If there is no quote, delete that section (and its rail entry) and renumber.

Never edit the raw numbers into approximations - use what the client verified. If a figure isn’t confirmed, leave the [X] placeholder rather than guessing.

Book a demo & Download PDF are built in at the foot of every case study - leave them in place; wire the form to your backend on integration.

House rules: keep realfast lowercase and italic everywhere, all headings and rubrics in sentence case (never all-caps), and use spaced hyphens, never em-dashes.

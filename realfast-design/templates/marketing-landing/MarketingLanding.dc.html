The problem

Most AI programmes stall one floor below production.

The demo works. The pilot gets applause. Then it meets the real workflow — the approvals, the exceptions, the four systems that disagree — and it does not survive contact.

The gap is not model quality. It is that nobody restructured the work around the model.

How we work

Three moves, six weeks, one number.

01

Restructure the work

We map the workflow end to end and decide what the model owns, what the human owns, and where the handoff sits. Two weeks, with your operators in the room.

02

Build in production

ExoCode ships against your real data in your environment from day one. No sandbox, no throwaway prototype, no second build to productionise.

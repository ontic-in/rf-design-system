The full blog article header — rubric, serif headline, standfirst, and a byline row (author avatar, name + role, date · read-time) with a Share control. Use inside `[data-theme="editorial"]`.

```jsx
<ArticleHeader
  section="Insights" sub="The realfast view"
  title="AI doesn't have bad taste. It's just missing constraints."
  dek="Teams are shipping internal tools faster than ever, and the interfaces all look subtly off."
  author="Manas Vaze" role="UX Design Lead"
  avatar="/authors/manas.png"
  date="Jul 13th 2026" readTime="5 min read"
  onShare={handleShare}
/>
```

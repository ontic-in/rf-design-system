UsageCharts sit on light grounds. Colour has one job per chart: identity (categorical), order (ordered), polarity (diverging) or emphasis (highlight). Validated for lightness, chroma, colour-blind and normal-vision separation. cat-2 and cat-4 sit under 3:1 on white, so print values or offer a table.
